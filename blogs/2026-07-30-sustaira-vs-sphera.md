As sustainability programs become increasingly strategic, organizations need technology that can support evolving regulatory requirements, enterprise-wide data management, and long-term ESG goals. While many platforms now offer sustainability capabilities, they differ significantly in their underlying architecture and the problems they were originally designed to solve. While Sustaira and Sphera are both enterprise sustainability platforms, they take very different approaches.
