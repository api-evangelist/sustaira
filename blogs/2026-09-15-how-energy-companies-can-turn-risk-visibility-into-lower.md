The energy sector is entering a period where uncertainty is becoming an operating condition, not an exception . Energy companies are balancing aging infrastructure, volatile markets, changing demand, geopolitical disruption, extreme weather, and regulatory complexity, while simultaneously investing in renewables, electrification, and decarbonization. The challenge is not a lack of data.
