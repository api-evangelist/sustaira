For a consumer goods company , sustainability does not stop at the factory gate. It starts with the ingredients you source, the materials your suppliers use, the packaging around your products, the energy consumed by manufacturing partners, and the thousands of decisions made across your value chain every day. That is what makes supplier sustainability so challenging for CPG companies.
